# My Favorite Movie: Grown Ups

## About
**Grown Ups** is a 2010 American comedy film directed by *Dennis Dugan*. Five childhood friends reunite after the death of their old basketball coach and spend a weekend together at a lake house with their families.

## Why I Like It
- Funny and lighthearted
- Great chemistry between the cast
- A reminder to value friendship and family

## Main Characters
1. Lenny Feder (Adam Sandler)
2. Eric Lamonsoff (Kevin James)
3. Kurt McKenzie (Chris Rock)
4. Marcus Higgins (David Spade)
5. Rob Hilliard (Rob Schneider)
